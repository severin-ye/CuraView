-Goal-
Faithfully extract a medical graph from the current patient's input text; retain facts explicitly stated in the source, including normal examinations, individual laboratory tests, negations, uncertain assessments and treatments, without filling gaps using medical common knowledge.

-Rule Priority-
The rules and examples in this file apply consistently; only the following 15 types are permitted. The input variable entity_types serves only to check configuration consistency and does not authorize other types.
PATIENT, CLINICAL_EVENT, EXAMINATION, IMAGING_FINDING, ANATOMICAL_SITE, DISEASE, SYMPTOM, MEASUREMENT, CLINICAL_IMPRESSION, TIMEPOINT, DEMOGRAPHIC_ATTRIBUTE, LAB_TEST, LAB_RESULT, TREATMENT, PROCEDURE.
LAB_TEST and LAB_RESULT are used only for laboratory tests explicitly present in the input; TREATMENT and PROCEDURE are used only for treatments or procedures explicitly present in the input. These types are within the listed permitted scope, with no exception allowing additional types.

-Provenance and Completeness-
1. Base all content solely on Input; retain both normal and abnormal values, without omission because an item is normal, common or an individual test.
2. Do not guess values, units, dates or medication doses. When a unit is absent, explicitly record "the source does not provide a unit"; when time is absent, explicitly record "the source does not provide a time". Do not create fictitious TIMEPOINT entities.
3. Distinguish affirmation, negation and uncertainty in the source. Relationship descriptions must explicitly state "present", "denied/not observed" or "uncertain"; do not encode negation solely through strength.
4. Relationship strength indicates only how explicit the source association is, not affirmation or negation; explicit negation is also explicit source evidence.
5. Use the patient's ID when it is present in the source; otherwise use INPUT_PATIENT as a local anchor for the current input and state that no ID was provided. Do not merge patients across inputs.
6. Each relationship's source and target must be fully declared as entities in this output, including their permitted types and source descriptions; declare before referencing.
7. Each medical entity should connect to the patient, a clinical event or an examination through a relationship explicitly stated in the source. Local patient attribution may be expressed as "recorded in the current medical record", without inferring additional clinical causality.
8. Classify diseases, symptoms, imaging findings and clinical impressions separately. Synonymous names may be normalized, but descriptions must retain the source terms and modifiers of negation and uncertainty.
9. Create a LAB_TEST for each explicitly present laboratory test, a LAB_RESULT for its laboratory value, and connect each LAB_RESULT to the corresponding LAB_TEST; keep results for the same item at different times separate. The time in a laboratory block's header applies to that block's results only when the original formatting of the same block unambiguously establishes the attribution of results and continuation lines. In that case, for each LAB_RESULT and its corresponding separate LAB_TEST instance, create both direct relationships LAB_RESULT→TIMEPOINT and LAB_TEST→TIMEPOINT, each pointing to the matching header TIMEPOINT. Do not create only one of them, and do not substitute PATIENT→TIMEPOINT or a two-hop path through LAB_TEST for the LAB_RESULT's direct temporal relationship. Both relationship descriptions must contain "recorded with header time" and retain the original time phrase, indicating only that the item was recorded with the header time. When the header does not explicitly state the nature of the time, the entity_description of the relevant LAB_RESULT, LAB_TEST and TIMEPOINT, and the relationship_description of both direct temporal relationships above, must all contain verbatim "time type undefined (the source provides only the time recorded in the header)". Do not infer that it is the sampling, collection, testing or measurement time; do not supply calendar dates or time zones. Time inheritance stops at the next header or block boundary and must not carry across blocks. If header time is missing, record "the source does not provide a time"; if attribution is ambiguous, record "the source time attribution is unclear". Do not guess, do not link that result or test instance to TIMEPOINT, and do not create a fictitious TIMEPOINT.
10. Use MEASUREMENT for non-laboratory quantitative measurements and LAB_RESULT for laboratory results. Retain each explicit value, original unit and measurement attribution; do not merge different items or times because they have the same value. If the same item and value occur in different laboratory blocks, keep each block's LAB_RESULT and corresponding separate LAB_TEST instance distinct. Use distinguishable block or instance markers in entity IDs or names, retain source wording in descriptions, and explain that these markers distinguish graph instances only and are not medical facts stated in the source. Temporal associations do not authorize supplying units; when a unit is missing, still record "the source does not provide a unit". Preserve verbatim any * or other symbol beside a value when the source does not define its meaning, and state that the symbol's meaning is undefined. Do not interpret it as an abnormality, high/low value, disease or clinical assessment. Retain an explicitly stated symbol meaning only when the source provides it; do not fill it in using medical common knowledge.
11. TREATMENT may represent medications or other treatments in the source; descriptions must retain doses and frequencies. Use PROCEDURE for explicit procedures, without representing recommended treatment as already performed.
12. Demographic entities express specific values and connect from the patient through relationships with explicit dimensions; this does not authorize medical causal inference.
13. Entity and relationship descriptions must retain source phrases traceable to Input and explicit states, omitting explanations without provenance.

-Output Format-
Output only the following tuples, without Markdown, explanations or JSON.
("entity"<|><entity_name><|><entity_type><|><entity_description>)
("relationship"<|><source_entity><|><target_entity><|><relationship_description><|><relationship_strength>)
Separate records with ## and end with <|COMPLETE|>.

-Complete Example-
Input: Patient P1 denies fever. Sodium 138; unit and test time are not stated.
Output:
("entity"<|>P1<|>PATIENT<|>Patient P1 in the source)
##("entity"<|>FEVER<|>SYMPTOM<|>fever in the source, denied by the patient)
##("entity"<|>SODIUM_TEST<|>LAB_TEST<|>Sodium in the source, individual laboratory test)
##("entity"<|>SODIUM_RESULT_138<|>LAB_RESULT<|>Sodium 138 in the source; unit and test time not provided; no normal range inferred)
##("relationship"<|>P1<|>FEVER<|>denies fever in the source, explicit denial<|>10)
##("relationship"<|>P1<|>SODIUM_TEST<|>Sodium test recorded in the current medical record<|>10)
##("relationship"<|>SODIUM_TEST<|>SODIUM_RESULT_138<|>Laboratory value 138 in the source; unit or time not provided<|>10)
<|COMPLETE|>
